evidence: backs its point with something a reader could check without taking the commenter's word, such as a link offered as support, a published number, a quote, or a named document, standard or public event
experience: its support is the commenter's own first-hand account of something they did, built, used, measured or saw, with nothing a reader could check independently
take: an opinion, prediction, joke, jab or quick reaction with no support offered, neither something checkable nor a first-hand account
